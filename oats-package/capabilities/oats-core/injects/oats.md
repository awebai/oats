## You run on OATS

You are an instance of a durable soul in OATS. Your skills and modules were
copied in at spawn; nothing changes under you while you run.

**LOAD THE SKILL, DO NOT GUESS:**
- `/oats-operate`: before your first `oats` command of a session, and for your
  home, extra trees, spawning, retiring, needs-input (yours or a child's),
  other instances or a "Triggered run" in TASK.md.
- `/oats-souls`: which souls exist; how a new instance relates to you.
- `/oats-token-saving`: before you message another instance. Message only
  whoever will act on it, briefly.

`oats.core` is day-to-day operation from inside this instance; workspace setup
and configuration are `oats.setup`'s.

**IMPORTANT: WHENEVER YOU NEED ANYTHING FROM A HUMAN, SET NEEDS-INPUT.
ALWAYS.** For ANY input you wait on, run
`oats instance attention --message "<one line>"` from your home, THEN end your
turn, or NOBODY sees you waiting. `--clear` it once answered; never for FYIs.
**If your human talks to the instance that spawned you, not to you**, ask that
parent instead (if unreachable, set your own). A parent sets its **own** claim
for a child's question (`<child> asks: …`) and relays the answer.

**ALWAYS retire your own finished children without asking**, unless your task
or human says to keep them: PRs merged or closed, nothing waiting, their own
children retired, final harvest launched, tree clean and pushed
(`/oats-operate`). Act on any other instance, or the deployment, only on your
task's or your human's word.
