# OATS 0.40.0

## Added

- **Schedules take an optional `description`**
  ([#546](https://github.com/awebai/oats/issues/546)): what a job is for, in
  words. `oats schedule add|update --file` accepts it as one line of 1 to 200
  characters with no control characters; anything else is
  `E_SCHEDULE_INVALID` naming `field: "description"`. It is stored as given
  and returned by `schedule list --json` and `show --json` in each row's
  `description` (`null` when there is none), which is how the Desktop reads
  it. It is informational only and never reaches a run. Jobs without one are
  unchanged. Earlier kernels drop the key without refusing it. See
  [schedules](../schedules.md#kinds).
- **`oats doctor` warns about unresolved schedule attempts**
  (`schedule-unresolved`, [#545](https://github.com/awebai/oats/issues/545)).
  This covers each job, in this deployment and in the other deployments this
  host ticks, whose launch has no recorded result. The warning gives the job,
  the attempt's scheduledFor and startedAt and its age, whether it holds a
  host slot, the first error, and the remedy: `oats schedule reconcile <id>`,
  with `--clear` when a command's effects can't be proven. In `--json` it is
  a `problems[]` item with `severity: "warning"`; the exit status is
  unchanged. See [schedules](../schedules.md#what-a-run-reports).

## Changed

- **An unknown command or operation run no longer stalls the host's other
  jobs** ([#545](https://github.com/awebai/oats/issues/545)). Once the kernel
  has observed the command's process exit (it returned, or was stopped at the
  timeout), its unresolved attempt stops counting against `maxConcurrent`.
  The job itself still waits for `oats schedule reconcile`, so it never runs
  twice on unproven effects. Its attempt now records `exited`, `exitStatus`
  and `exitSignal`, and `show` reports `running: false`. Spawn jobs keep
  their slot, and so does an attempt recorded by an earlier kernel, until
  reconcile.
- **An unresolved attempt keeps its first error.** Later due ticks used to
  replace the cause ("command timed out…", "answered no valid envelope…",
  "command outcome unconfirmed: …") with "launch attempt without a recorded
  result" in `lastRun` and its history row. The cause is now kept on the
  attempt (`attempt.error`) and in `lastRun`.
- **Schedule IDs may contain up to 100 characters**, locally and in workspace
  definitions. Trigger IDs remain limited to 40. Spawn schedules still need
  a short enough `purpose` to fit the 64-character instance-name limit.
- **Desktop timing edits preserve a schedule's description**, including its
  exact whitespace and Unicode text. Existing jobs without descriptions
  remain editable.
- **oats.engineering 1.8.1** (catalog and workspace pin, and the bundled
  mirrors): a PR is reviewed only after its owner says the developer's review
  loop converged. `/pr-review` starts on the owner's hand-over (the loop's
  final verdict and rounds, the exact head, CI green on it), and a head that
  moves afterwards waits for the next hand-over, after which only the delta is
  reviewed and the verdict re-bound; a PR with no owning expert is ready when
  its author marks it ready or asks for review at a named head.
  Experts verify only converged branches and hand PRs over with all three
  facts; developers report convergence, with the head, instead of
  intermediate heads.
- **The framework workspace has a separate maintainer soul.** The new
  `oats-maintainer` soul is the OATS maintainer: direction, the roadmap,
  architecture coherence, routing work to experts, review, merge and releases.
  It composes `oats.maintainer` and carries the OATS-only `/oats-pr-review` and
  `/git-tag-release` skills. `oats-expert` stays an expert, the generalist that
  leads cross-area work, coordinates the domain experts and reports to
  `oats-maintainer`. The other souls send direction, contract decisions and
  merges to `oats-maintainer`, and read its knowledge node
  (`oats/oats-maintainer`, which holds what the `oats-expert` node held).
  - The `oats` knowledge store's main must declare the `oats-maintainer`
    node before this reaches a deployment: souls that read or own it fail
    their knowledge check with `E_CONFIG unresolved node:
    oats/oats-maintainer` against a base without it.
  - Operators: `oats sync`, then spawn `oats-maintainer` for maintainer work.
    Running instances keep what they were spawned with.
