# OATS 0.29.3

## Changed

- **There is no "personal team"; a workspace has its default team.** The
  vocabulary follows the teams contract's amendment of 2026-09-26: a workspace
  has its **default team** (the team for the workspace key in its owner's
  namespace), and agents may join other teams. The model is unchanged:
  - an instance is in the workspace's default team only, by default;
  - joining an eligible team is an explicit act;
  - `OATS_TEAMS` is `[]` for a soul with no label.

  The kernel's docs, comments and the `unmapped-team-label` warning use the
  new words. The warning now reads "… fall back to the workspace's default team
  for it". The oats.framework skills (oats.core 2.1.1, oats.setup 2.1.2,
  oats.framework 1.3.1) teach the same model. The kernel carries no wire name
  with "personal", and there are no compatibility aliases.
- **The messaging provider uses the same words** (oats.aweb 1.16.0, below).

## oats.aweb 1.16.0 (catalog pin and bundled mirror)

- **The catalog and this repo's workspace pin `oats.aweb` to `v1.16.0`, and
  the bundled `capabilities/oats-aweb` is the tag's tree.** It renames the
  provider's wire names to the default-team vocabulary:
  - the teams operation's `personal` field becomes `defaultTeam`
    (`{ team, source }`, where `source` is `setting` or `root`);
  - `E_TEAM_PERSONAL` becomes `E_TEAM_DEFAULT`;
  - the broker receive label `personal` becomes `default`;
  - `settings.oats.aweb.roots.personal` is removed: `roots` keys are team ids
    only, and the `personal-root-deferred` warning is gone.

  There are no aliases: a reader of the old names must move to the new ones,
  as the Desktop does in this release. A home spawned under 1.15 still resolves
  its default team from its recorded team. Per-workspace default-team
  enrollment is still deferred.

## oats.framework 1.3.1 (catalog pin)

- **The catalog and this repo's workspace pin `oats.framework` to
  `oats-framework/v1.3.1`.** Its only change is the default-team vocabulary
  above, in `oats.core` 2.1.1 and `oats.setup` 2.1.2. A deployment on
  `oats.framework: v1.3.0` keeps working, and moving the pin to `v1.3.1` picks
  up the new skill text.

## Desktop

- **"Default team" everywhere** (#252). The teams panel reads oats.aweb 1.16's
  `defaultTeam` and says where the team comes from: the workspace or host
  setting, or the messaging root's active team. The spawn dialog, the soul
  inspector and the teams panel say "Default team" in place of "Personal".
  A 1.15 `personal` document no longer decodes, so this Desktop needs the
  oats.aweb 1.16.0 pin.
- The Desktop still accepts OATS CLIs `>=0.25.8 <0.30.0`.
