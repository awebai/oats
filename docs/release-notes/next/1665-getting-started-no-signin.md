## Changed

- **Getting started creates a new workspace's aweb account and team with no sign-in.** By default, `oats-getting-started` no longer asks the person to sign in to aweb in their browser. Step 1 names the account `N` (default `<workspace>-oats`) and its root `A` (`<workspace>-root`). The skeleton's team is `default:N.aweb.ai`. Step 9 creates the account and team with `oats aweb setup --soul S --username N --name A --label L`, plan first, and goes on only when the answer's `result.team` equals that line and `result.membershipVerified` is true. When `N` is taken, the person picks another, the team line is changed, committed and synced, and the signup runs again. The person is told the team is on an aweb account created for the workspace, which they attach themselves to in the setup expert's first steps (`/oats-tailor-workspace`). Signing in with `aw auth login` and `aw team ensure`, for a team owned by their own aweb account from the start, is now the optional step 5 ([awebai/oats#1665](https://github.com/awebai/oats/issues/1665)).
