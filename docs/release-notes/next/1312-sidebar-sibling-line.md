## Changed

- **The Desktop sidebar draws the sibling line apart from the lines to
  children** ([#1312](https://github.com/awebai/oats/issues/1312)). The
  dotted line joining sibling instances runs through their dots, and a
  parent's line to its children runs just to the right of it, so the two no
  longer share one line. The dotted line is darker in the White and Solarized
  themes, so it stands out on every row (3:1 contrast). See
  [the sidebar's instance tree](../desktop.md#the-sidebars-instance-tree).
