## Provider updates

- **oats.framework 1.16.2: oats.core 2.9.1.** oats.core's `/oats-operate`
  and `/oats-souls` now state spawning authority the way its "You run on
  OATS" inject already does. You spawn when your task, your human or your
  soul's capabilities say so, so a developer spawning its reviewer, or an
  expert its developers, needs no human. When a relation is unclear, ask
  your parent or the agent that gave you the task first, and your human
  only when no agent can say (#1621). This is text only. The floors are
  unchanged, and the other capabilities are unchanged.
- **Upgrade:** `oats sync` locks it once the workspace or the catalog pins
  `oats.framework: v1.16.2`. New spawns get it, and a stopped home moves with
  `oats instance upgrade`, from OATS 0.68.0.
