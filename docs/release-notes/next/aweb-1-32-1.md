## Provider updates

- **oats.aweb 1.32.1: recover pi mail lost on an interrupted turn.** The
  catalog and this workspace pin `oats.aweb: v1.32.1`, from 1.32.0. It is
  guidance only, cut from 1.32.0 with this one change: with `@awebai/pi`
  0.4.0, mail that arrives during a turn can be lost when the human
  interrupts it (awebai/aweb#68). The inject and `/oats-aweb` section 4 now
  have affected pi instances scan `aw mail inbox --show-all --json` after any
  interrupted turn and at task boundaries, and reconcile by message id. The
  workaround goes once a fixed `@awebai/pi` is pinned. OATS >=0.68.0 and the
  aw floor are unchanged.
