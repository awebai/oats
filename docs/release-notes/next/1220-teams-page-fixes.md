## Fixed

- **Desktop: a team card's warning can be copied.** On the Teams board, the
  text of a team's warning or blocking problem, with its fix, can be
  selected and copied, and a click on that box no longer opens the team's
  page. A click anywhere else on the card still opens it, and the card is
  still one Tab stop.
- **Desktop: a Make default confirmation closes with the team's page.** A
  confirmation opened on a team's page no longer stays open after Back, Esc,
  a tab change or the team going away, and the Teams board updates again
  when your instances change.
- **Desktop: a failed read shows on an open team page.** When the teams on
  this computer can't be read, the open team's page says so in its card, in
  the board's words, instead of showing facts that may be out of date.
