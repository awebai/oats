## Changed

- **Desktop: switching back to a workspace puts the keyboard back where you
  left it.** The terminal you were typing in has the focus again, ready to
  type, with no click; so does the instance's row in the list of instances
  (once the list is read), the tab, or the page's control. Picking a workspace
  from the list of workspaces, a move (Alt+↑ / ↓, ⌥↑ / ↓, the leader's
  ↑ / ↓) and a window falling back after another window took its workspace
  over all do it. A move made from the list of workspaces leaves the focus on
  the list. A tab that closed meanwhile gives the focus to the tab active in
  its place, or to the page
  ([desktop.md](../desktop.md#one-window-per-workspace)).
