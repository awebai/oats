## Changed

- **oats.core: spawning a child your capabilities call for needs no human.**
  `/oats-operate` and `/oats-souls` said to spawn only when your task or your
  human asks, and to ask your human when a relation is unclear. They now admit
  your soul's capabilities as authority, as the oats.core block already does
  (a developer spawning its reviewer, an expert its developers), and send an
  unclear relation to your parent or the agent that asked you first, and to
  your human only when no agent can say. This matches oats.engineering's
  automatic retirement, where spawning and retiring never wait on a human.
