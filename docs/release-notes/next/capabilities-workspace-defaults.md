## Added

- **Desktop: Workspace › Capabilities shows the workspace's defaults.** A new
  **Workspace defaults** section, above Workspace owned, shows what every soul
  starts with unless it turns one off:
  - **Core capabilities**: Knowledge, Messaging and Tasks, at most one of
    each per soul. A filled slot shows the capability and where it comes
    from, and opens its page. An empty one says there is no workspace
    default and each soul chooses its own.
  - **Additional capabilities**: any number per soul, the other capabilities
    the workspace gives every soul, shown the same way. One the workspace
    turned off is struck through.
  - A workspace with no defaults, such as a standalone deployment, says so in
    one line. The search and the repository pills never hide the section,
    and the section jump starts with **Defaults**. With an OATS that does not
    report the defaults, the section is not shown.
- **Desktop: "What capabilities are".** The round **i** button after the
  section jump explains what a capability gives a soul's instances, the three
  places one comes from, which capabilities are core and which are
  additional, and what workspace defaults are and why they matter.
  Workspace › Souls has the same button, **About souls**, which explains what
  a soul is
  ([Desktop](../desktop.md#capabilities-and-spawning)).

## Changed

- **Desktop: the Workspace tabs read Souls, Capabilities, Automations, Teams,
  Setup.** Teams was first, and Automations, its own page before, is a tab.
  The page still opens on Souls, and the arrow keys, Home and End follow the
  new order
  ([desktop-keyboard.md](../../packages/desktop/docs/desktop-keyboard.md)).
