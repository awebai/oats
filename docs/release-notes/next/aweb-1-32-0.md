## Provider updates

- **oats.aweb 1.32.0: stopped seats upgrade in place, and joined teams mint
  only from a member root.** The catalog and this workspace pin
  `oats.aweb: v1.32.0`. It requires OATS >=0.68.0; the aw floor is unchanged.
  - **`oats instance upgrade` moves a stopped seat** (local, global grant or
    retained, including 1.17.1 metadata) to the locked package. Plan is
    read-only. Apply keeps the identity and grant, and makes no host
    registration or `aw` call. The next start re-registers the home and, where
    configured, renews the grant before revoking the old one. That ends the
    lost wakes after an upgrade or a restart: a grant seat's new grant home is
    registered first, and a local Codex seat recorded under `delivery: channel`
    registers `<home>/.aw` as a session home. `oats.aweb-tasks` adopts with it.
  - **A team other than the default** is joined at spawn, and its roster read,
    only from `roots[team]` or `root`, and only when `aw team list --json`
    there lists the team. The cwd, `OATS_WORKSPACE` and the home's own `.aw`
    never mint for it. A missing or non-member root refuses, naming the team,
    the root tried and the remedy (`oats aweb setup --join <label>
    --invite-stdin`, then a respawn). `oats aweb join --labels L --plan`
    shows the root a join would use, without inviting.
  - **Claude channel:** a start clears a cached aweb-channel failure, which
    made every Claude started in the next 15 minutes skip the channel, and
    readiness reads Claude's MCP log to prove a home registered on the channel
    (`channel-not-connected`, with the restart remedy).
- **Upgrade:** `oats sync` locks it; new spawns get it, or a stopped home
  moved with `oats instance upgrade`.
