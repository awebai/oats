## Provider updates

- **oats.okf 5.1.0** (from 5.0.5), mirrored from the published `v5.1.0`
  tag at `40265ac79eca54a76ae648462dbfc83ac370dbbe`. The mirror, catalog and
  workspace pin move together; the mirror and inventory are generated by
  the parity checker from that tag.
  - **A harvest survives its proposer's retirement.** The required spawn
    hook verifies the source's live record or event-named retirement recovery,
    then atomically captures the proposal, source declarations/bindings and
    only named backing notes in the harvester's own home. Later retirement
    or recovery disposal cannot remove captured input. Missing/ambiguous
    provenance still refuses; soul opt-out, unrelated harvesters, the no-wait
    rule and reviewed acceptance remain unchanged.
  - **Requires OATS >=0.49.0**, where the public source-event lookup first
    works after home removal. Upgrade the kernel before the provider pin;
    re-spawn harvesters for the new hook and instructions. Restarting keeps
    a home's copied provider. The onboarding skill now names 5.1.0 and this
    kernel floor; that skill change ships with the next framework release.
  - **Binding URL sequencing:** change a binding's `repository` to the plain
    URL only after every reading seat has been re-spawned on oats.okf 5.0.4
    or later; a session restart does not upgrade its copied modules.
  - Source capture refuses reused/ambiguous source incarnations and incomplete
    event histories. Legacy note references are preserved or explicitly
    refused, never silently dropped or replaced by a shorter filename.

  Provider changes: [oats-okf#82](https://github.com/awebai/oats-okf/pull/82).
  The generated mirror/pin change is not evidence of deployment adoption or
  a model-run harvest; existing homes and deployments remain unchanged until
  explicitly upgraded.
