## Changed

- **Instances are named for their work, with the soul's role last.** `/oats-operate`
  and `/oats-souls` (oats.core) teach `--name <descriptive>-<role>`, the role being
  the last word of the soul's name: `--name desktop-improvements-expert` for an
  `oats-desktop-expert`, not `oats-desktop-expert-desktop-improvements`. `--purpose`
  still derives `<soul>-<purpose>`, for automations and scripts that know only the
  soul. [Souls and instances](../souls-and-instances.md) states the convention.
