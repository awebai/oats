## Changed

- **Desktop: the instance panel starts closed and stays as you leave it.**
  Opening an instance's terminal (from the sidebar, the palette, the overview,
  Quick Open or a tab switch) no longer opens the panel on the right. Whether
  it is open is remembered on this computer: open it once (its shortcut, the
  palette's **Instance panel: show / hide**, or **Show instance panel**) and
  it stays open for every instance until you close it. The Workspace view's
  soul inspector still opens when you select a soul.
- **Desktop: a closed sidebar or panel leaves nothing behind.** The thin strip
  left by a hidden sidebar and the narrow rail of a collapsed panel are gone:
  the terminal gets the whole width. To open them again, besides their
  shortcuts and the palette, the top bar has **Show sidebar** at its start
  while the sidebar is hidden or focus mode is on, and **Show instance panel**
  at its end while the panel is closed. Show instance panel carries the
  Developer page's dot when that page has changes.
