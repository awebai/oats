# OATS 0.29.1

## Changed

- **The `oats-setup-admin` soul is never harvested.** Its `soul.yaml` opts out
  of OKF harvest with `knowledge: { harvest: off }`: its sessions work on the
  deployment's own config and carry deployment specifics that must not reach
  the shared knowledge base. oats.okf 4.0.0 makes a soul's opt-out absolute, so
  a deployment that switches harvest on (`settings.oats.okf.harvest: on`)
  still registers no source, captures nothing and takes no custody for its
  instances. `oats okf harvest-status` in one of its homes reports
  `harvest: off` with the soul's reason.
