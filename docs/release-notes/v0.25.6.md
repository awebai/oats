# OATS 0.25.6

The kernel half of the per-spawn identity choice (local | global). No new CLI
flags; three provider-neutral additions and one manifest attribute.

## Added

- **`decision.effective.providers`**: the spawn decision binds the merged
  per-module payloads the providers will receive (the preview's
  `settings.<cap>`), so a confirmed apply covers every provider fact by value.
- **`hostOnly` settings keys**: a manifest may mark a settings key `hostOnly:
  true`, accepted only from the deployment's `oats-local.yaml`
  `settings.<cap>` and refused elsewhere (`E_WORKSPACE_SCHEMA`, reason
  `host-only-key`), so a committed file cannot point a messaging provider at a
  custody root.
- **Served identity on the roster**: `oats status --json`
  `instances[].identity`, the roster's `identity:` line and `oats inspect
  --home … selected.identity` carry the principal an instance acts as, from its
  messaging provider's hook meta. Feature `served-identity`. Any messaging
  provider that emits `identity` in its spawn or launch meta appears there.

## Desktop

- **Terminal owner leases** (#117, `terminalApi: 2`): every terminal is a
  lease held by the admitted renderer document; a navigation, reload or
  renderer crash revokes it and cleans its viewers; the 20-terminal cap cannot
  be overshot by parallel opens; writes are bounded and one-way. See
  [terminal owner leases](../../packages/desktop/docs/terminal-owner-leases.md).
