# OATS 0.69.4

Unreleased

0.69.4 ships the new coordination skills. Finished instances retire
automatically, coordinated between agents without a human
(oats.engineering 1.17.2). An overrun maintainer gets an overflow seat. And
oats.core says a soul's capabilities can call for a spawn (oats.framework
1.16.2). There is no kernel or Desktop change beyond the version.

## Upgrade

- **Install the Desktop first and the CLI second,** as
  [Upgrading](../upgrading.md) says. The 0.69.4 Desktop accepts OATS CLIs
  `>=0.26.0 <0.70.0`.
- **Package moves:** the official catalog pins oats.framework 1.16.2 and
  oats.engineering 1.17.2 (Provider updates, below). A deployment takes them
  with `oats sync`.
- **Running instances keep the skills they were spawned with.** New spawns
  get the new skills. A stopped home moves to them with
  `oats instance upgrade`, and its session restarts.

## Changed

- **oats.core: spawning a child your capabilities call for needs no human.**
  `/oats-operate` and `/oats-souls` said to spawn only when your task or your
  human asks (or a documented workflow requires it), and to ask your human
  when a relation is unclear. They now also admit your soul's capabilities, as
  the "You run on OATS" block already does (a developer spawning its
  reviewer, an expert its developers), and send a question about an unclear
  relation to your parent or the agent that gave you the task, and to your
  human only when no agent can say. A capability that spawns its own helpers
  no longer reads as needing a human for it.

## Provider updates

- **oats.engineering 1.17.2: finished instances retire automatically, and an
  overrun maintainer gets an overflow seat.** The catalog and this workspace
  pin `oats.engineering: v1.17.2`. The floor is unchanged.
  - **Automatic retirement** (oats-engineering#28). An instance is finished
    when three things hold:
    - its PRs are merged and green, or closed (handed back is not landed);
    - nothing waits on it;
    - its final harvest is launched.

    It then sends its parent a done line, and its parent retires it after
    checking that the work tree is clean and pushed and that nothing is
    attached, without asking or telling a human. A developer retires its
    reviewer, an expert its developers, a maintainer the leads it spawned. An
    instance with no OATS parent retires itself. Maintainers are standing
    seats and are never retired this way.
  - **Overflow seat** (oats-engineering#29). A maintainer with more than 5
    efforts of its own in flight spawns one overflow seat as its child. The
    seat drives the new intake up to hand-back. It holds no entry on the lane
    map, and merges, second reads and sign-offs stay with its parent.
  - 1.17.1 and 1.17.2 fix two lines of 1.17.0. An instance with no parent
    retires itself with `oats retire "$OATS_INSTANCE" --self`, because the
    kernel requires the name. The harvest condition names no knowledge-layer
    command: your knowledge layer says whether you have a harvest duty.
- **Upgrade:** `oats sync` locks it. New spawns get it, and a stopped home
  moves to it with `oats instance upgrade`.

- **oats.framework 1.16.2: oats.core 2.9.1.** oats.core's `/oats-operate`
  and `/oats-souls` now state spawning authority the way its "You run on
  OATS" inject already does. You spawn when your task, your human or your
  soul's capabilities say so, so a developer spawning its reviewer, or an
  expert its developers, needs no human. When a relation is unclear, ask
  your parent or the agent that gave you the task first, and your human
  only when no agent can say (#1621). This is text only. The floors are
  unchanged, and the other capabilities are unchanged.
- **Upgrade:** `oats sync` locks it once the workspace or the catalog pins
  `oats.framework: v1.16.2`. New spawns get it, and a stopped home moves with
  `oats instance upgrade`, from OATS 0.68.0.
