# OATS 0.38.1

## Changed

- **oats.aweb 1.20.0: `oats aweb roster` lists who is on the team and says
  what each entry is** (awebai/oats-aweb#46). The roster printed only the
  team's membership certificates (`aw id team members`). That listing omits
  identities the registry does not list, such as a team's retained global
  coordinator and dashboard humans. It also listed deployment roots and
  certificates with no workspace exactly like agents. The roster is now the
  union of those certificates and the team's workspace presence
  (`aw workspace status`), read from the minting root. Each entry has its
  sources (`certificate`, `presence` or both), its status (active, when last
  seen, or `certificate only: no workspace record`) and a kind:
  - `global identity`, from the identity scope, listed first;
  - `human` and `hosted agent`, from the session context;
  - `instance` and `deployment root`, from the workspace path;
  - `unknown`.
  An inferred kind says so, and nothing is called retired. aw does not mark a
  team's coordinator, and the roster says so. When a source cannot be read,
  or presence is past its 200-workspace cap, the roster still lists what it
  has and says it is incomplete. `oats aweb roster --json` is now an oats.aweb
  document (`{team, members, certificatesComplete, presenceComplete,
  problems}`) instead of aw's raw listing. The oats-aweb skill, the inject
  and the spawn brief say what the roster shows and what it can't tell you.

## Fixed

- **The Desktop opened from Finder no longer shows `/` as its workspace**
  (awebai/oats#518). A Finder launch starts in `/`, and when no saved
  workspace was a deployment, the Desktop served `/`. It titled the window
  `/`, showed `E_NO_DEPLOYMENT` in Instances, and remembered `/` in
  `windows.json` for every later launch. A folder that is not a deployment is
  now never served, bound to a window or saved. With no deployment to open,
  the window opens on the workspace switcher, which lists the deployments
  directly inside `~/Agents` under "On this computer": one click opens one in
  that window. A window remembered on `/` is forgotten at launch.

- **A window with no workspace lists the workspaces served after it opened**
  (awebai/oats#521). Its choices were fixed when it opened, so remote
  workspaces arriving a few seconds after launch never appeared and the
  switcher said "No workspace choices reported."

- **Copying an agent's output in the Desktop** (awebai/oats#520). A plain
  drag over a terminal now selects the text (tmux's copy mode, so it can
  reach into the scrollback) and copies it to the clipboard on release;
  ⌘C, Edit › Copy and the right-click menu find it there. The Desktop takes
  tmux's copies (OSC 52) write-only: nothing in a terminal can read the
  clipboard. Remote terminals (`oats session attach --server`) bind the same
  drag. ⌘V is unchanged; Option-drag still makes a terminal-only selection.
