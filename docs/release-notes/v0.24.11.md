# OATS v0.24.11 — retirement recovery: kernel-owned fields only, nothing re-blessed after launch

Kernel/Pi/Desktop **0.24.11**. **Upgrade from 0.24.10: it can lose an agent's
early home bytes at retire.**

## Fix

0.24.10 made a fresh keyed spawn retire clean by re-hashing the whole home after
the kernel's own post-spawn writes (completion marker, wake record). That
re-hash ran after launch, so anything the agent wrote in between (`STATE.md`,
notes) was absorbed into the retirement baseline and **not recovered** at
retire.

The re-hash is gone. The baseline fingerprint hashes `instance.json` with only
the kernel-owned post-spawn fields (`spawnCompleted`, `wake`) removed; nothing
else in the home is re-baselined, and the home is not observed after launch.
Bytes written at launch are now recovered under `changed instance-home bytes`;
a plain keyed spawn still retires with `workRecovery: null`.

## Upgrade

`npm i -g @awebai/oats@0.24.11`, then `oats doctor`.
