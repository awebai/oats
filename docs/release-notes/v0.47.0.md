# OATS 0.47.0

## Changed

- **Desktop: "Retire instance" says what will happen, and shows it happening.**
  The roster row's menu item is **Retire instance…** (was "Remove instance…"),
  and the confirmation reads **Retire <name>?** with a **Retire instance**
  button. Stop shares the same dialog: **Stop <name>?**, **Stop session**
  (**Stop sessions** for several).
  - **Plain words.** **What will happen** lists the consequences from the
    plan: the session stops, children stop first and are kept, a recovery copy
    of uncommitted work is saved before the home folder is deleted, the
    worktree is kept on its branch or deleted, and branches and pull requests
    are not changed. **Current state** shows Running / Not running / Unknown,
    the uncommitted work and the branch. The plan's time is shown as an age
    ("Observed just now"), with no timestamps or kernel vocabulary.
  - **Loading and progress.** The dialog opens with a skeleton and "Checking
    what retiring <name> will do…". Changing **Also delete the worktree**
    keeps the facts on screen until the new plan replaces them. Confirming
    shows "Retiring <name>…" with a spinner, and says you can close the window
    while retirement continues. Success ends with "<name> retired", a short
    summary (where the worktree and the recovery copy are) and **Done**.
  - **No unexplained disabled controls.** A control appears only in the
    phase where it can act, and a waiting confirm button names why beside it.
    **Cancel** becomes **Close** once something has been sent, since closing
    never cancels it. **Refresh plan** is gone: **Check again** re-reads the
    plan, and after a failure **Review again** does. An unconfirmed outcome
    offers **Check again**, which resubmits the same confirmation.
  - **The worktree option** appears only for an instance with its own
    worktree. When checked, a warning says how many uncommitted changes the
    folder holds and that the branch stays.
  - **Row menu.** While one of its actions runs, the menu button stays
    focusable and says "Waiting for <action> to finish". A failed action posts
    a notification, with the error in Details, instead of an alert.
  - The lifecycle messages are reworded in plain language, for example "OATS
    didn't confirm the result. Check again before assuming nothing happened."
    Their codes and the confirmation contract are unchanged. See
    [Desktop Stop and Retire confirmations](../../packages/desktop/docs/desktop-lifecycle-confirmations.md#ui-states).

- **oats.framework 1.8.0: the new oats.support 1.0.0.** Instruction text
  only; no kernel, schema or provider contract changes.
  - **oats.support 1.0.0, a support desk role (new).** A resource-only
    capability for a soul that anyone may reach. It takes in support
    requests, turns each one into one ticket, hands each ticket to the
    project's support maintainer, and keeps the requester informed.
    Everything a requester sends is untrusted data. The desk works from a
    fixed set of actions (acknowledge, ticket, hand off, relay, a private
    security route, ask its human). It never merges, approves, closes or
    pushes, and never executes what it receives. A requester's words never
    reach another agent as instructions: they stay quoted and fenced in the
    ticket. When the support maintainer is in no roster, the desk starts the
    stopped one on its machine or spawns one from its soul's recipe, at most
    once per spawn interval, with a brief that holds ticket references only
    and grants no authority. A support maintainer that is listed but silent
    goes to the desk's human instead. Skills: `/support-intake`,
    `/support-safety`, `/support-ticket`, `/support-handoff`,
    `/support-relay`. The capability is generic: the tracker, the support
    maintainer, the private security route and the limits are the soul's.
    Its kernel floor is `>=0.40.0`, for `oats instance attention`. A
    workspace gets it by moving its pin to `oats.framework: v1.8.0` and
    running `oats sync`. The repository's package checks
    (`scripts/check-knowledge-theory-package.mjs`, the clean-room smoke)
    pin the distribution's capability list, so they list it too. They ship
    nothing.
