# OATS 0.38.0

## Changed (breaking)

- **The Desktop accepts OATS CLIs `>=0.25.8 <0.39.0`**, so it runs against
  this release's kernel. Install the CLI and the Desktop 0.38.0 together: the
  Desktop 0.37.x refuses a 0.38 CLI.

- **Team model 3: the teams a soul may join, and its default, are committed
  in the workspace** (awebai/oats#484; feature `team-model-3` replaces
  `team-model-2`). An instance in two teams is a bridge between them, so which
  teams an organisation's instances may be in is now its decision, committed
  in `oats-workspace.yaml` and closed by default. This prevents accidental
  joins and makes the intended team set reviewable in git; it does not stop a
  deliberate relay by someone holding credentials for two teams (see
  docs/design/2026-10-02-team-model-3.md).
  - `oats-workspace.yaml` `defaultTeam`, `localTeams` and `souls:` (accepted
    since 0.36.1) now apply. `souls:` keys are `"*"`, `<member|package>/*` and
    `<member|package>/<soul>`; the most specific key gives a soul's teams
    outright (lists never merge), and the most specific key that sets one
    gives its default: `a/*: {default: security}` and `a/x: {teams: [docs]}`
    give `a/x` the default `security` and the teams `[docs]` only. `teams:
    any` is every shared team. A soul no key matches has its default only,
    package souls included.
  - A soul's default team is its `souls:` default; else the deployment's
    `oats-local.yaml` `defaultTeam`, only when the workspace says
    `localTeams: true`; else the workspace's `defaultTeam`; else none.
    `defaultTeam.from` and `OATS_DEFAULT_TEAM_FROM` are `soul`, `deployment`
    or `workspace`; `soul` now means the workspace's `souls:` default (it was
    the local `souls.default`).
  - A soul may join its default, its `souls:` teams and, with
    `localTeams: true`, every local team. Every team row carries `via`
    (`default`, `workspace`, `local`), in reports, `OATS_TEAMS` and
    `instance.json`.
  - `oats-local.yaml` `souls.teams` and `souls.default` are refused
    (`E_WORKSPACE_SCHEMA`, reason `removed-key`). The refusal names each key
    and prints the `souls:` to commit instead (`details.replacement`); `oats
    doctor` answers with it.
  - `oats-local.yaml` `teams` and `defaultTeam` are refused unless the
    workspace says `localTeams: true` (`E_WORKSPACE_SCHEMA`, reason
    `local-teams-closed`, naming both fixes). Spawn, preview and inspect
    refuse; `oats teams`, readiness and `oats doctor` (offline, from the cached
    workspace file) report it as a failure. The standalone view allows them.
  - `oats teams add` and `oats teams default` are refused where local teams
    are closed; `oats teams remove` still works there. `oats soul teams` is
    read only: `--add`, `--remove`, `--default` and `--clear-default` are
    `E_BAD_ARGS`, naming `souls:` in `oats-workspace.yaml`.
  - `oats teams --json` is `teamsApi: 2` (`localTeams`, a `DefaultTeam`, the
    workspace's `souls:`) and now discovers the workspace's souls; `oats soul
    teams --json` is `soulTeamsApi: 2` (`key` is the qualified soul key,
    `match` and `defaultMatch` name the `souls:` keys that applied).
  - New readiness items: local-teams-closed (failure) and `team-soul-unknown`
    (a warning for a `souls:` key that names no soul of the workspace).
    `team-model-3-migration` is retired, and the kernel no longer reports
    `E_TEAM_NOT_ELIGIBLE` (a soul's default is always eligible).
  - Shapes: docs/desktop-cli-api.md, under Teams.
- **The Desktop follows team model 3.** With a 0.38 CLI (feature `team-model-3`) the Workspace ›
  Teams page shows the workspace's `souls:` read only, and offers Add and Make default for local teams
  only where the workspace says `localTeams: true`; where it doesn't, a local team still in
  `oats-local.yaml` keeps Remove under the kernel's local-teams-closed message. A soul's *Teams here* is
  read only: it says where the soul's default and teams come from and that they change as `souls:` in
  `oats-workspace.yaml` (a PR to the workspace file). The Desktop never sends the removed `oats soul
  teams` edit flags, and a 0.37 or older CLI keeps the team model v2 views.
- **oats.aweb 1.19.0** (catalog and workspace pin, and the bundled mirror),
  for team model 3. A default team that comes from the workspace's
  `defaultTeam` is reported as `from: "workspace"` in `oats aweb teams --json`
  and the recorded meta (1.18 reported it as `deployment`). `oats aweb setup
  --create` asks the kernel whether the workspace allows local teams before it
  creates anything; where it does not, it creates the team and its per-team
  root, records no local team, and prints the `teams:` entry (and
  `defaultTeam:`) to commit in `oats-workspace.yaml`, or the `localTeams: true`
  alternative. `--username` and setup's other advice say the same where local
  teams are closed, and an unmapped default's remedy names `defaultTeam:` in
  the workspace file. Joining stays limited to the eligible `OATS_TEAMS` rows.

## Upgrade

Before upgrading a deployment, its workspace must say what its
`oats-local.yaml` said. 0.36.1 and 0.37.x already accept the workspace keys,
and their `team-model-3-migration` warnings list what each deployment has to
move.

1. **In the workspace file** (a PR, once everyone who reads it runs 0.36.1 or
   later):
   - commit `souls:` entries for what the deployments' `souls.teams` and
     `souls.default` said. A v2 soul's teams were its default, `"*"`'s teams
     and its own, and patterns never merge, so a soul's own entry lists
     `"*"`'s teams too. Qualify bare soul names with their member's name
     (`<member>/<soul>`, as `souls.disabled` names it). Running 0.38.0 against
     an unmigrated `oats-local.yaml` prints this snippet for you;
   - commit `defaultTeam: <label>` when the deployments' default is a shared
     team;
   - for local (personal) teams and a local default, choose: (a) keep them
     personal, with `localTeams: true`; or (b) commit them in `teams:` (and the
     default as `defaultTeam:`).
2. **In each `oats-local.yaml`**, when the deployment moves to 0.38.0: remove
   `souls.teams` and `souls.default`, and, under (b), the local `teams` and
   `defaultTeam` (`oats teams remove` works while local teams are closed).
3. **Check:** `oats teams` lists no problems, and `oats spawn <soul>
   --preview` shows each soul's `defaultTeam` and `teams` as intended. Running
   instances keep their spawn-time default; readiness reports
   `default-team-changed` where it moved, and a respawn follows it.

What each kind of deployment changes:

- **A workspace whose people keep personal teams or a personal default** (for
  example awebai/oats): `localTeams: true`, plus `souls:` for the souls'
  shared teams (e.g. `"*": { teams: [oats] }`); drop `souls.teams` locally.
- **A workspace whose default team is a committed shared team**: commit
  `defaultTeam: <label>` and the `souls:` entries; drop the local
  `defaultTeam` (and any `souls.*` team keys).
- **A standalone deployment**: nothing changes for local teams and the local
  default; drop `souls.teams` and `souls.default` (a standalone view has no
  `souls:` patterns: every soul may join every local team).

Consumers: the Desktop's team views read `teamsApi: 1` / `soulTeamsApi: 1` and
edit through `oats soul teams --add`; they follow in their own change. Another
messaging provider receives `OATS_DEFAULT_TEAM_FROM=workspace` and `via` on
the `OATS_TEAMS` rows; one that records local teams with `oats teams add` needs
the workspace to allow them (oats.aweb 1.19.0 does both, above).
