# OATS 0.27.1

## Changed

- **oats.aweb 1.14.2 is bundled and pinned** (was 1.13.1): **joining teams
  beyond the personal one now works.** An instance joins the eligible teams it
  is given at spawn (`--provider oats.aweb join=<labels>`) or later with
  `oats aweb join <labels>` / the Desktop's Teams controls, and leaves with
  `oats aweb leave <labels>`. Each joined team gets its own identity under the
  instance home. Joined teams **poll** (their mail is read between tasks); live
  receive for joined teams is planned for oats.aweb 1.15. Joining needs
  **aw 1.36.12 or later** (older aw answers `E_TEAM_AW_FLOOR`; the primary
  identity still mints). A leave removes the local identity only after aweb
  confirms the membership is released, so a failed leave can be retried.
  Retire leaves every joined team before releasing the primary identity.
- The framework workspace (`oats-workspace.yaml`) pins oats.aweb v1.14.2.
- Skip oats.aweb 1.14.0 and 1.14.1 (tagged, never pinned by an OATS release): their binding check answered a `teams` key that OATS rejects, so `oats readiness` showed messaging unknown; 1.14.2 fixes it, and 1.14.0's joins were refused by aw.

## Known limitations

- A per-workspace personal team still needs oats.aweb 1.15 (the aweb service
  and CLI already carry personal enrollment); until then "personal" is the
  person's active aweb team.
