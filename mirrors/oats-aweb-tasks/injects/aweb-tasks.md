## Tasks: aweb

Your tasks layer is `oats.aweb-tasks`. Load `/aweb-tasks` before finding,
claiming, updating or handing off work. Run its commands from your instance home,
using your own oats.aweb identity in the default team; joined teams use their
explicit identity home. If you have no oats.aweb identity, ask your human rather
than creating one. The human is an assignee like any other team member: resolve
their alias from the roster, never guess it. Task state belongs here;
conversation belongs to the deployment's messaging layer.
