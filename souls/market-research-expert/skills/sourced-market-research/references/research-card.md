# One attributable claim

Use one card per independently checkable claim. Blank or unknown is preferable to guessed metadata.

```text
Question / decision:
Product:
Version or edition:
Deployment mode:
Tested task / comparison scope:
Claim:
Source URL:
Source revision or exact section:
Short supporting passage or permission-safe paraphrase:
Publication date (if known):
Retrieved at (actual date):
Evidence kind (first-party assertion / independent observation):
Reproduction method and outcome (if tested):
Confidence and reason:
Counterevidence:
Unknowns / limitations:
Recheck trigger:
Owner:
```

Keep source fact, analyst inference and recommendation in separate fields or paragraphs. A failed source lookup is not evidence the feature does not exist. Prefer a brief attributed paraphrase over copied proprietary text; exclude credentials, private customer identifiers and raw correspondence.
