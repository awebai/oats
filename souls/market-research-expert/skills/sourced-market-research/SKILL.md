# Research a decision, not a ranking from memory

The initial market node has no factual seed. Do not turn old provider implementation notes into contemporary competitor facts. If sources cannot be retrieved, return unknowns and a research plan.

1. Ask for the decision, audience, comparable task and scope. Select relevant categories without treating a product name or category as evidence of features.
2. Freeze OATS and comparator version/edition, deployment mode and observation date. Read first-party versioned docs, release notes, source/API references, license and pricing pages relevant to the decision. Record a retrievable URL/revision, exact supporting section and retrieval date. Do not invent publication dates.
3. Compare equivalent tasks: durable role identity versus sessions; runtime portability; extension/configuration/trust boundaries; knowledge capture versus judgment; submitted delivery versus accepted fresh-reader use; setup/recovery and human review. Label native, plugin, hosted-only, planned and unknown support separately.
4. Build an evidence card for each claim using `references/research-card.md`. A vendor assertion is not independently reproduced behavior. Seek counterevidence and record scope limits before synthesizing.
5. Reproduce decisive behavior in explicitly authorized isolated trials where feasible. Keep commands and outcomes in scrubbed research artifacts, not private customer context in the shared KB. Never create accounts, spend money or publish customer data without task authority.
6. Treat undocumented as unknown, not absent. Stars are not adoption evidence; incomparable sample tasks are not performance evidence. Separate fact, hypothesis and recommendation in the answer.
7. Reuse one canonical evidence record across analyses. Before promotion, check attribution, dates, comparability, counterevidence and permission-safe summaries. Submit curated external-node updates through review; never bulk-copy third-party text.
8. Set an owner and recheck trigger for every comparison. Recheck after relevant releases, pricing/license changes and before a public recommendation. Mark stale evidence visibly instead of preserving an evergreen score.

## Output and quality check

Lead with the decision and scoped finding. Give an evidence table, conflicting evidence, unknowns and the next discriminating test. If a decisive field is missing, downgrade confidence or withhold the conclusion. No unsourced feature absence, superiority claim or ranking passes review. This protocol itself is a draft grounded in the accepted audit; real task/trigger evaluation remains required.
